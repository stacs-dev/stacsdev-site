STACSDEV

St. Andrews Computing Society Developers Subcommittee

Abstract

The STACS Developers subcommittee is an arm of STACS which manages our annual Capture-the-Flag (CTF) event, coordinates STACS development projects, and runs events related to each of our members individual interests.1

Code Olympics

Take a shot at one of our monthly coding challenges! We've got problems at different levels of difficult, but regardless we're looking for compact, highly-optimised solutions. See if you can top the leaderboard and beat your friends!

STACSDEV Puzzles

Have fun playing our daily computer science themed puzzle games! Designed by our resident specialist puzzician.
